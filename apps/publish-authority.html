Who can publish into your build?

Accounts that can publish into this tree

Ordered by how much of the tree each one reaches. This is not a list of suspects. One person looking after a family of small, focused modules produces exactly this shape, and is usually the reason those modules work. What the numbers describe is how much of your tree would fail together.

Packages only one account can publish

A single maintainer is the npm norm, not a defect. What this marks is the absence of a second pair of hands: nobody else to notice an unexpected release, and nobody else able to publish a fix if the account is lost.

What this cannot tell you. npm publishes no two-factor status, and nothing that distinguishes a person from a team credential or a CI identity. So this describes the shape of the surface and never the strength of any lock on it. The maintainer list is as the registry reports it today, which is not who held the rights when you installed. And a package's declared repository is a claim the package makes about itself, unverified by npm.
